Prep notes
  1. Say it out loud. Reading an answer you recognise is not the same as producing one.
  2. Reach for trade-offs. Most of these have no single right answer; name the conditions that change it.
  3. Tie it to your own work. Interviewers follow up on whatever you claim to have built.
  4. Ask what is fixed. Clarifying constraints is part of the answer, especially on the system-design questions.
